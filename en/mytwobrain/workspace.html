Original and translation

BabelVoice — voice in the workflow

One hotkey dictates into the active field in a CLI or application. Transcription and translation connect speech with text, documents and conversation.

Hotkey→BabelVoice→Active field in CLI / APP

Dictation and transcription

Speech and audio recordings become text: a message, note or saved material.

Translation

A language route connects speech recognition, translation and the required output: text, subtitles or voice.

Duplex scenarios

My microphone → translation → voice. The other person's audio → text and subtitles. Each direction has its own settings.
