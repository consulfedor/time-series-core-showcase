Human / AI / Shared work

MyTwoBrain

From intent
to a result.

I build an environment for people and AI to work together: understand complex questions faster, keep important details in view and make well-founded decisions.

Attention is human time directed towards a purpose. Relevant context, knowledge and tools stay close at hand; less time goes into searching, switching and explaining again. The reasoning remains available for review and future work.

An idea, a deliverable and experience

The seed is a request or an idea grounded in knowledge and intuition. Work enriches it with data; in a shared focus, we make sense of the material, structure it and check it. The product can be handed over: a letter, document, code or decision.

The prism represents the shared focus of human and AI attention; the crystal within it represents evolving understanding and ways of working. Together they form the image of MyTwoBrain. The assembled fullerene represents material we have made sense of: some informs the next task, some is retained as experience. The gold added to the crystal is knowledge gained from successes and mistakes.

Experience becomes part of MyTwoBrain: its knowledge, skills, standards and agreements. Its relevance may become clear later. The next idea starts with this understanding.

A letter takes shape and is sent. The reply brings new information; reflection refines the preparation of future letters. Both the handed-over product and the change in how we work remain.

Interaction / Attention / Context

Everything for the work.
In one context.

The interface follows the thought: read and compare, discuss by voice, sketch a diagram, run a calculation and produce a document. Material stays beside the discussion; agent states become visible when oversight is needed.

01

Focus

The goal, the subject of work and the next step stay in view. The session plan and agent states help keep track of the question that needs a decision now.

02

Concentration

The dialogue and its material share one screen, reducing switches between windows. Documents, diagrams, drawings, tables, presentations and Markdown stay beside the discussion; history preserves the thread of work.

03

Context quality

Relevant, current and verifiable grounds for the next action. Translation, Wiki, ontology and thesaurus connect terms to their meaning; source links and versions support checking the reasoning.

What makes context useful

One source: the original and its owner are identifiable; copies link back to them.

Relevance: material addresses the current question and the next action.

Structure: terms, documents and their connections can be found and understood.

Sufficiency: there are enough grounds for a decision; missing information is identified.

Consistency: agreements fit together; contradictions remain visible until resolved.

Freshness: the version and date show which information applies now.

Verifiability: facts, assumptions and conclusions are distinguishable; sources can be checked.

Access: people and agents receive material within their permissions and confidentiality boundaries.

04

Follow the work

Agent states, changes and notifications show where the process is and when human attention is needed.

CLI / Zellij / Web UI

Execution on the server.
The interface in your browser.

CLI tools work with files, scripts and models on the selected server. Zellij keeps the terminal session independent of an open browser tab. The web interface brings the conversation, material and terminal to a laptop, tablet or phone.

Time series / Analysis / Forecasting

Understand what is happening.
Anticipate what comes next.

I look at processes as time series: measurements linked to time. Documents per hour, a pulse, motor current and load, temperature, market prices.

These processes unfold at the same time. What matters is which properties we observe, how we collect them and how we use their history to estimate what may happen next.
