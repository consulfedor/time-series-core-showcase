In one context.

The interface follows the thought: read and compare, discuss by voice, sketch a diagram, run a calculation and produce a document. Material stays beside the discussion; agent states become visible when oversight is needed.

Time series / Analysis / Forecasting

Understand what is happening.
Anticipate what comes next.

I look at processes as time series: measurements linked to time. Documents per hour, a pulse, motor current and load, temperature, market prices.

These processes unfold at the same time. What matters is which properties we observe, how we collect them and how we use their history to estimate what may happen next.

01

Quality data

Continuous collection, completeness, consistent timestamps, relevant measurements and a traceable source. RAW and aggregates preserve the detail the task needs.

02

Analysis and models

Trends, seasonality, dependencies, noise and changes of regime. Features, algorithms and experiments that capture the structure of the process.

03

Forecast and decision

A forecast horizon, likely outcomes and an uncertainty range. Evaluate on the next period and compare the forecast with what actually happened.

I build the path from data collection and recovery to features, algorithms and evaluation of forecasts.
