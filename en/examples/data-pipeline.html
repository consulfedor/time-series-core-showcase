Engineering case / data and reliability

The service is running.
But can you trust the data?

In my own project, I built a market-data workflow: ingestion, storage, calculation and historical-data checks. Detecting a gap and restoring the basis for further work is a separate task.

The task

Analytics needs a continuous time series. After a disconnection or restart, the database may respond while still holding incomplete history.

Service availability, data freshness and coverage of the requested range are therefore checked separately.

My contribution

Pipeline architecture, data lifecycle, recovery, checks and the operator interface. I develop with AI agents, tying requirements and acceptance to observable results.

This case concerns data infrastructure. It does not demonstrate trading rules or profitability.

The path of one record

Each layer has its own job

01 / Source

WS / REST

Live events and retrieval of the required historical range.

02 / Buffer

Redis

An operational data window for current processing.

03 / History

MongoDB

Base and calculated records, timestamps and coverage checks.

04 / Consumers

Analytics / ML

Verified history, derived datasets and Parquet snapshots.

Source and quality checks connect these stages. A record's presence does not establish its fitness for a subsequent calculation.

History coverage on one screen

The deliverable

A data range with known provenance, completeness checks and the status of derived calculations. An accompanying report supports continued work or investigation of a failure.
