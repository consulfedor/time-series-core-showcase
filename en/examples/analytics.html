Market analysis tools

An event on a chart should lead to an explanation: what was detected, on which data, what checks support it and what still needs a human decision.

In my own analytics project, I am developing an interface for this kind of review. The chart is linked to an event card, source data, calculation results and verification records. An automatically detected candidate and an accepted result are shown separately.

The review workflow

  1. Select an event and the relevant time window.
  2. Open its card with the explanation and calculation.
  3. Compare the automated conclusion against source data.
  4. Record a decision or leave the case under review.
  5. Return to the result together with its conditions and version.

Development: the research cycle

Data → method version → training → validation on the next time period → execution → comparison and conclusions.

The journal records experiment conditions and comparison results. This helps distinguish a correctly executed calculation from a useful improvement. A negative result is retained too: it rules out a hypothesis that has already been tested.

What this example demonstrates

  • Connecting visualization, calculations and manual review.
  • Separating source data, candidates and confirmed decisions.
  • Versioned research experiments and explainable comparisons.
  • A clear path from an overall report to an individual event.
