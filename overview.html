English summary

MyTwoBrain is my private human–AI engineering workspace: a conversation next to documents, files, plans, agents and verification results. The public demo shows the interaction with fictional data; it does not connect to the private system or an LLM. See the technical guide.

Fact → Decision is a construction-company AI adoption concept. Its interactive foundation example connects a drawing, 3D model, quantities, schedule, cost, deliveries and an evidence-backed decision package. It is a proposal and demo, not a claim of a completed enterprise deployment.

Repository and local preview

This repository is the public portfolio surface, not a mirror of private implementation code. It contains the website, public explanations and approved demonstrations. No dependencies or build are required for the website:

python3 -m http.server 8080 --bind 127.0.0.1

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080/. The workspace demo also opens as a local HTML file. The C1 presentation is a self-contained generated artifact; its provenance is recorded in the release manifest. Research drafts, personal conversations and original screenshots stay outside this repository.
